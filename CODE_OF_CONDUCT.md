# Code of Conduct

ScaffoldScope welcomes technical disagreement and expects professional conduct.
This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and project-related communication.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss claims, code, and evidence rather than a contributor's identity or
  presumed motives.
- Be precise about uncertainty, limitations, conflicts of interest, and data
  provenance.
- Give actionable review feedback and allow reasonable time for a response.
- Respect privacy, embargoed vulnerability reports, and repository access
  boundaries.
- Credit prior work and correct attribution errors promptly.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, intimidation, sustained disruption, fabricated evidence,
credential disclosure, and retaliation against a good-faith reporter are not
accepted.

Strong criticism of a method or result is allowed. Personal abuse is not.

## Reporting

For an immediate public-thread problem, stop engaging and flag the comment to the
maintainer. For a private report, use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/satwiksps/scaffoldscope/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `Conduct report:`. Do not include the report in a public
issue.

Include links or screenshots, the relevant dates, and any safety concern. The
maintainer will limit disclosure to people needed to assess the report, will hear
the affected parties where safe, and will document the reason for an enforcement
decision privately.

## Enforcement

Responses may include a private warning, removal of content, a temporary pause in
participation, or a permanent ban. Severity, repeated behavior, safety, and the
need to protect the integrity of the project will guide the response. Knowingly
false reports may themselves violate this policy; an unproven good-faith report
does not.

The project maintainer is responsible for enforcement. If the maintainer is the
subject of a report and no independent project representative is available, use
GitHub's platform reporting tools.
